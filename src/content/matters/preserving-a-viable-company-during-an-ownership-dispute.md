title: Preserving a viable company during an ownership dispute
category: Business Litigation

summary: >-
  Two equal owners reached an impasse over compensation, management authority, and the future of a profitable company they had built together.
outcomePreview: >-
  A negotiated separation allowed one owner to acquire the other's interest while the business continued operating without interruption.
situation: >-
  Two equal owners of a regional service company had reached a point where they could no longer agree on compensation, hiring authority, distributions, or the long-term direction of the business. Both remained essential to operations, but continued deadlock was affecting employees and delaying important business decisions.
approach: >-
  Rather than treating the dispute only as a lawsuit between owners, the strategy focused on the value of the operating company and the consequences of allowing the conflict to continue. Financial records, governing documents, ownership rights, and potential claims were evaluated alongside possible separation structures.
outcome: >-
  The parties negotiated a structured buyout allowing one owner to acquire the other's interest over time. The company continued operating throughout the transition and avoided a forced shutdown.
